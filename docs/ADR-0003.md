# ADR-0003: Technical claims and evidence linking

**Date:** November 2, 2026. **Status:** Local development implementation.

## Why
A list of vaguely related research results is insufficient to evaluate an innovation. Every startup claim needs a specific description of the mechanism, linked original sources, and a reviewable comparison by a person. General ideas, patentability and the business moat are different questions.

## Decision
Use two new SQLite tables: claims and claim_sources. Claim records are scoped to the author's project. A claim-source mapping links an existing source and a claim in the SAME owner-scoped project, with a relation enum (overlap, possible-difference, background, uncertain), optional manually entered source excerpt and rationale. Explicit links and notes persist across future research runs and are included in exports. All mutations validate project ownership and source membership. Foreign-key cascades remove mappings with deleted source/claim.

Researchers may manually attach an HTTPS public patent publication, company documentation, or a research article. These are marked manually supplied and not independently verified; the application does NOT fetch, validate publication number, determine legal status, or infer whether any source anticipates a claim. This workflow does not constitute patent searching or legal analysis.

## API
- GET/POST /api/v1/projects/:id/claims
- PUT/DELETE /api/v1/projects/:id/claims/:claimId
- GET/POST /api/v1/projects/:id/claims/:claimId/sources
- DELETE /api/v1/projects/:id/claims/:claimId/sources/:evidenceId
- POST /api/v1/projects/:id/evidence for public manual source registration

## Subsequent gate
Provider-backed patent bibliographic data and publication-number search; primary text/family claims; chunk-level retrievability; independently evaluated recall and citation correctness; review workflows that require source dates and qualified patent counsel. No automatic "patent approved" or percentage-original result.
